Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-06

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and the profile you choose to fill in. Login is by Google Firebase; we never see your password.
  • Listings: what you publish, screenshots, and proof of ownership (GitHub repository facts, domain DNS check).
  • GitHub: if you connect it, your GitHub username and an access token, stored encrypted. We use it only to read: to prove you own a repository and build its health report. Disconnect any time.
  • Deals: offers, messages, handover progress, and the payout details you give us (stored encrypted, seen only by our team).
  • Security logs: important actions with IP address, to prevent fraud.

Who sees what

Buyers and sellers never see each other's email address; messages go through SecondLife. Your public profile shows only your display name, bio, website and GitHub username if connected.

Who else handles it

Google Firebase (login), GitHub (if you connect it), Escrow.com (deals), Postmark (emails), Anthropic (writing the health report summary from repository facts), and our hosting provider.

Your rights

Download your data or delete your account in Profile. Completed deals are kept for accounting, with your personal details removed.
