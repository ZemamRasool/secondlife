1. What SecondLife is

SecondLife is a marketplace where people sell and buy unfinished software projects, domains and small websites. We are not a party to the sale. We check ownership proof, run escrow through a licensed escrow company, and provide the handover checklist.

2. Sellers

  • You may only list things you own, or have the right to sell. You confirm this for every listing.
  • Describe the project honestly, including what is missing or broken.
  • Hand over everything listed after the escrow is funded, and only then.
  • Never ask a buyer to pay you outside SecondLife's escrow.

3. Buyers

  • Pay only into the escrow named on your deal page. Never pay a seller directly.
  • Check the handover and confirm, or open a dispute, within 7 days of the seller finishing it. If you do neither, the money is released to the seller.

4. Fees

Listing is free. When a sale completes, the seller pays a 10% commission, taken from the sale amount. Escrow company fees are shown by the escrow company before you pay.

5. Disputes

If the buyer opens a dispute, the money stays in escrow while we review the evidence from both sides. We may decide to release the money to the seller or return it to the buyer, within the escrow company's own rules.

6. What we may remove

We may remove listings and close accounts that break these terms, including stolen code, misleading listings and attempts to take payment outside escrow.

7. Liability

Projects are sold as they are. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the commission we received on the deal in question.
